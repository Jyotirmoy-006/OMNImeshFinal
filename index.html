Executive Summary

The Test

We evaluated decentralized edge Max-Pressure traffic signal control across 17 signalized intersections in OMNImesh during a 3-hour morning peak (06:00–09:00, 21,600 to 32,400 seconds) over 14 simulation random seeds, benchmarking against SUMO's native fixed-time plans and isolated actuated control.

The Outcome

Max-Pressure achieved substantial congestion relief, reducing corridor trip delay by 257.29 seconds (-27.0%, SD 57.96 s) and network-wide delay by 145.09 seconds (-17.9%, SD 40.67 s) compared to fixed-time signals. Actuated control provided intermediate performance, trimming corridor delay by 108.31 seconds.

The Limits

Four pre-registered claims were unmet: emergency ambulances still face delays during peak congestion (-21.5% travel time reduction vs 40% target), severe camera sensor failure costs 13.9 seconds without coordinated fallback, single-junction fallback is inadequate to prevent cross-street spillback, and the 150-second waiting-time guard adds 5.2 seconds of network-wide delay to protect diamond interchanges against starvation.

Deployment Prerequisites

Three requirements remain before physical deployment: reliable roadside vehicle-detection cameras, a robust low-bandwidth inter-node communication link (0.15 kB/s per intersection), and hardware-in-the-loop tests with the city's physical controllers.

Caveats & Known Limits

  • Single morning demand pattern: The evaluation models a single 3-hour morning peak (06:00–09:00); evening peak, off-peak, and incident traffic conditions remain unmeasured.
  • Camera fault levels are assumed: Sensor failure rates and outage schedules are model assumptions, not empirical field failure distributions.
  • Raspberry Pi factor is assumed: Single-board microcomputer execution latencies assume an extrapolated 10x multiplier over workstation benchmarks rather than physical bench tests.
  • BOM prices unverified: The $213.64 roadside hardware bill-of-materials reflects estimated component catalog list prices without procurement or installation labor quotes.
  • 3D Unity scene shows older network: The interactive 3D visualization displays an earlier network geometry with pre-timed signals and does not run the adaptive AI controller.

Quick Terms

Seed: A random starting value creating natural variations in vehicle departures, routing, and lane selection across simulation runs.
Pressure: The difference between the number of queued vehicles approaching a signal and available capacity on receiving lanes downstream.
Teleport: SUMO's automated removal and re-insertion of a vehicle when it has waited longer than 300 seconds at a red light or gridlock.
Trimmed Mean: The average trip duration after removing the slowest 5% of trips to filter out extreme congestion spikes.

Corridor-wide fallback protected both median (103.18%) and slowest trips (trimmed mean retention 100.93%, trimmed mean difference -1.91 s vs Ideal).

Methodological Caveats & Limitations

  • Single Morning Peak Only: All simulations model a single 3-hour morning peak window (06:00:00 to 09:00:00). Off-peak, evening peak, and weekend dynamics are unmodeled.
  • Identical Demand Across Seeds: The identical 11,341-trip synthetic origin-destination profile is applied to every seed; variability is purely stochastic in vehicle insertion and turn choices.
  • No Dynamic Vehicle Rerouting: Vehicles in these simulations follow pre-assigned paths and do not dynamically divert to alternate routes when queues form.
  • Teleports Occurred Across All Controllers: Teleports occurred in every controller due to SUMO's 300 s safety rule (1 to 24 teleports depending on controller and seed).
  • Engineering Assumptions: Camera outage levels (25% moderate, 50% severe), single-board 10x compute scaling factor, and hardware bill-of-materials costs are engineering assumptions, not field measurements.
  • No Physical Field Deployment: All findings derive from microscopic traffic simulation; no physical intersections were actuated.
  • 3D Unity Scene Network: The 3D Unity scene visualizes an earlier network iteration running fixed-time signals with no adaptive AI controller active.
